# Simulmedia

> Simulmedia is a technology-driven TV advertising company. Its platforms (Performance TV, the TV+ media-buying platform, and Simulmedia Self-Serve) let advertisers plan, buy, and measure campaigns across linear TV, connected TV (CTV), and games. Through PlayerWON, Simulmedia also runs an in-game rewarded-video engagement and monetization platform for free-to-play console and PC games, backed by a developer API.

This llms.txt was generated by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline from the Simulmedia public developer surface and the first-party PlayerWON SDK.

## Products
- [Performance TV](https://www.simulmedia.com/): Tech-powered TV ad planning and buying for advertisers and agencies.
- [TV+ media-buying platform](https://www.simulmedia.com/): AI-powered platform for linear and CTV campaign planning, activation, and measurement.
- [PlayerWON](https://www.playerwon.com/): In-game rewarded-video engagement and monetization for free-to-play games.

## API
- PlayerWON API — base URL https://game.simulmedia-apis.com — OAuth2 client-credentials; brokers rewarded-video ad opportunities into games.

## SDKs
- [PlayerWON SDK (Unity, Unreal, Go reference server)](https://github.com/simulmedia/playerwon-sdk): First-party game-engine SDK for integrating PlayerWON opportunities.
